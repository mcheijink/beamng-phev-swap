# Changelog

## 1.0.1 (not released yet)

- The car now starts in Boost: on spawn, after replacing the vehicle and when the ignition comes back on. The drive-mode selector, its on-screen button and the traction and stability settings start in Boost too. A vehicle reset still keeps the mode you chose.
- Plug-in Hybrid Monitor: a full 10 or 18 kWh pack showed 6.7 / 6.8 and 12.1 / 12.2 kWh (the stored energy rounded down, the capacity up). The usable kWh are now rounded to 0.01 before they are shown to 0.1, so a full pack reads 6.8 / 6.8 and 12.2 / 12.2 kWh.
- Standstill charging (about 1,800 rpm and 11 kW in P or N, about 2 kW in D) is no longer marked provisional: the figures are kept as they are.
